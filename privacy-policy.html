3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only for the purposes stated at the time of collection:

  • Responding to enquiries — we will reply to your message via email or phone as indicated.
  • Scheduling product demonstrations — if you request a demo, we will use your contact details to arrange it.
  • Follow-up communication — with your prior consent only. You can opt out at any time by replying "unsubscribe" to any email or by contacting us at support@prosight360.in.
  • Site security — server logs are used to detect and prevent abuse.

We do not use your data for automated profiling, targeted advertising, or resale to any third party.

4. Data Storage & Security

All personal data collected through this website is processed by FormSubmit.co (a form delivery service) and delivered to our secure email. FormSubmit processes form submissions to facilitate delivery and does not store your data indefinitely.

Our product platform (Prosight One + Command Center) stores all footage and operational data on AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1) — 100% Indian data residency with zero cross-border transfer. This applies to all data collected through ProSight deployments, not just this website.

Security measures in place for this website include:

  • HTTPS with HSTS (Strict-Transport-Security) enforced across all pages
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) restricting external script execution
  • X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN — prevents clickjacking
  • X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff — prevents MIME-type sniffing
  • Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin

7. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions for 24 months from the date of receipt — long enough to support any follow-up related to a sales conversation or product pilot. After 24 months, data is deleted from our email systems unless an active customer relationship exists.

You can request deletion of your data at any time — see Section 8.

8. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India), you have the following rights as a Data Principal:

Right to Access
Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
Right to Correction
Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
Right to Erasure
Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
Right to Grievance Redressal
Lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer (see below) — we respond within 7 working days.
Right to Withdraw Consent
Withdraw consent for data processing at any time — withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing.
Right to Nominate
Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@prosight360.in with the subject line "DPDP Rights Request". We will respond within 7 working days.
